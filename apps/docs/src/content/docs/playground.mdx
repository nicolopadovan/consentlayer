This page runs consentlayer with three providers that only log what the
manager asks them to do:

| Provider | Needs |
|---|---|
| `cookieless` | nothing (`'none'`) |
| `product-analytics` | analytics |
| `ads-pixel` | marketing |

Choose in the banner, change your mind with **Cookie settings**, and track a few
events. The log shows each hook as it runs, newest first: nothing reaches a
provider before its category is granted, `load` runs once, and withdrawal runs
`teardown`. Your choice is stored under `playground_consent`, so it survives a
reload.
